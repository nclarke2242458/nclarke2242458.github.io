Niamh Clarke is an MA candidate in the Teaching Literature and Writing concentration and is pursuing a Graduate Certificate in Digital Humanities. Her current research interests include nineteenth-century Irish and British Gothic fiction; monstrosity and confessional politics in religious-minority writing; publication anonymity, editing, revision, and adaptation; computational literary studies, including corpus building and authorship attribution, and its relation to close reading and textual history.
